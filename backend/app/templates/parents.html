A plain weekly note about your child

Know how your child's preparation is going without needing to understand the syllabus. Written in English, Hindi or Telugu, and always about your own child only.

Create a parent account

How to link your child

  1. Ask your child for the family code

    It is on their Sahayak home screen, six letters and digits.

  2. Create your account

    Enter your name, an email, a password and the family code. You can add more children later with their codes.

  3. Read the note

    Pick your language. The note says what is going well, what needs more time, and when the next test is.

What you see, and what you do not

  • You see: a short note, the test date and chapters, and minutes practised each day for the last week.
  • You do not see: marks, rank, comparison with classmates, or any other child.
  • Your child can remove every parent's access from their own screen at any time.

Hindi and Telugu notes are drafts that should be reviewed by a native speaker. How data is handled
