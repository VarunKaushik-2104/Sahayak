Find the gap behind the wrong answer

Sahayak is a free adaptive learning app for students in Classes 6 to 10. It reads which mistake a student made, follows it back through earlier topics, even from earlier classes, and practises exactly that gap.

Maths and Science for CBSE (NCERT), ICSE and the Telangana State Board. It works offline after the first visit.

A wrong answer is information

A mark of 4 out of 10 says a student struggled. It does not say why. Every wrong option in Sahayak's {{ n_questions }} questions was written to match one specific misunderstanding, so each answer tells the app what to practise next.

It finds the root, not the symptom

A student stuck on quadratic equations may really be missing polynomials from Class 9 or linear equations from Class 8. Sahayak follows its prerequisite map of {{ n_topics }} topics back until the gap is found.

It fits how classes are taught in India

Chapters follow the NCERT, ICSE and Telangana State Board lists. Students prepare for the unit test their teacher has set, by date, then take a mock test.

It keeps working without data

The syllabus and questions are stored on the phone. Answers are saved first and sent to the teacher when the phone is next online.

How a student uses it

  1. Take the 5-question placement check

    It starts at medium difficulty and gives a first picture of what the student knows across topics, including earlier classes.

  2. Practise a chapter

    Questions get easier or harder as the student answers. A wrong answer with a known cause sends the student to the earlier topic behind it, then back.

  3. Prepare for the test

    The revision plan counts the days to the teacher's test date and puts the weakest basics first. A timed mock test closes the loop.
