Home / For teachers

See the gaps in your class before the test

Students practise on their own phones, even offline. You get one view of who is ready for your test, which basics are missing, and what to teach or set next.

Create a teacher account

Set up in three steps

  1. Create your account and school

    Sign up with your email and your school's name. Sahayak gives your school a student code and a separate staff code for colleagues.

  2. Give students the school code

    Students type it when they create a profile, or add it later from their home screen. They appear on your dashboard as soon as they join.

  3. Choose the test

    Pick the board, class and subject, then the test date and chapters. Every student's revision plan and every parent's note follows it.

What the dashboard shows

Class heatmap

Mastery for each student by chapter or by topic, with a "test ready" column. Prerequisite topics from earlier classes appear next to the chapters that need them.

Groups

Students are placed in Extend, Practise or Basics from their numbers. You can move anyone, and send practice to a group's phones.

Morning briefing

A short list of what to do first: the topic most of the class is missing, who has not synced for days, and who is ready for more.

Worksheets

Built from the gaps found in each group for the test you set.

After the test

Which chapters to revisit with the whole class, based on the same data.

Your school only

You see only students who joined with your school's code. Students never see each other's results.

Before you rely on it

Questions and lessons were written for this project and reviewed, but a teacher should read them before using them with a class. Chapter lists should be checked against the current year's syllabus. See About for what has and has not been tested.
